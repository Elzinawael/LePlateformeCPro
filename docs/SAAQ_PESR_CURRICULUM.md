# SAAQ PESR — reference curriculum

ConduiPRO's default class 5 curriculum follows the SAAQ's official Automobile (passenger vehicle) PESR outline:
https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/permis-conduire/cours-conduite/automobile

## Structure
- 4 phases.
- 12 theory modules, 2 hours each (24 hours total).
- 15 on-road outings, 55 minutes each (15 hours total).
- Minimum phase durations stated by SAAQ: 28, 28, 56 and 56 days.

## Phase and module distribution
| Phase | Official name | Modules | Outings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Préalable au permis d’apprenti conducteur | 1–5 (theory + school evaluation) | — |
| 2 | Conduite dirigée | 6–7 | 1–4 |
| 3 | Conduite semi-dirigée | 8–10 | 5–10 |
| 4 | Conduite semi-dirigée à autonome | 11–12 | 11–15 |

## Permit milestone correction
The SAAQ states that the learner's permit follows completion of the first four theory modules and passing the driving school's theory evaluation (module 5) in Phase 1. The permit is required before Phase 2 road outings. Therefore, ConduiPRO gates permit upload on completion of Phase 1 module 5, rather than Phase 2 module 5.

## Automated onboarding
New students receive the default Phase 1 assignment and progress rows for all theory modules and practical outings. School-specific curriculum phases override the global templates phase by phase.
